labyrinth-selfcheck: is the desktop in a sane state? Run it after an upgrade (or when something looks off).

  labyrinth-selfcheck            print a list of checks: ok, warn or FAIL, with what to do
  labyrinth-selfcheck --quiet    print only warnings and failures; exit status 1 on any FAIL, 2 on a warning

What it looks at (read-only, nothing is changed):
  * the session needs a log out: Hyprland started before it or Yggdrasil's session, lock screen or panel were last
    upgraded (a lock screen newer than its session can fail to start: this really happened under Plasma on 2026-10-16)
  * the running kernel is not the newest installed one (reboot)
  * Hyprland reports the screen(s), and the panel is running
  * greetd is the login screen and its greeter is installed
  * who owns the notification service (should be the Labyrinth panel)
  * Labyrinth's services (Ollama, Limbo, Heimdall, Sarina, per-app network and power limits) and failed units
  * Heimdall answers, and its file-integrity watch covers the binaries installed from the AUR
